FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are your legal documents legally binding? +
Yes. All documents are drafted by qualified attorneys and are legally valid and enforceable when properly completed and executed according to relevant jurisdiction requirements.
Can I modify a document after downloading it? +
Absolutely. All documents come in both PDF and editable Word format. You can customise any section — we recommend having a solicitor review significant modifications.
Which jurisdictions do you cover? +
We currently cover all 50 US states, the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. Documents are jurisdiction-specific and updated regularly.
Is there a free trial available? +
Yes — 7-day free trial on all plans. No credit card required. Download up to 3 documents during the trial.
How is my data kept secure? +
256-bit AES encryption for stored data, TLS 1.3 in transit. We never share your documents or personal information with third parties.

What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), as well as PayPal and bank transfers for Enterprise plans.

Our Story

About Virtual Legal Advisor

We believe quality legal advice should be accessible to everyone — not just those who can afford expensive law firms.

Our Mission

Making the law work for everyone

Founded in 2018, Virtual Legal Advisor was born from a simple frustration: legal help was either too expensive, too slow, or too complicated for ordinary people and small businesses.

We built a platform where anyone can access professionally drafted legal documents, guided by smart technology and backed by a team of qualified attorneys — at a fraction of the traditional cost.

Today, we serve over 50,000 clients across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. Our library covers 200+ document types, all kept current with the latest legislation in every jurisdiction we operate in.
